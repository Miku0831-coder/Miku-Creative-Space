Hatsune Miku (Miku V6 Original, Miku V6 Soft & Miku V4X Original) — MIKU NO MONO NI NARU NO NE? — Miku 19th Anniversary

Happy Birthday Hatsune Miku!

Happy Birthday my sweet Miku!

I love you so much!

It’s one of the anniversary songs I created for you, Hatsune Miku, in celebration of your 19th anniversary.

I truly hope you enjoy it.

It’s part of a three‑version series of the same song.

This one is the Japanese version, sung by Miku V6 Original, Miku V6 Soft, and Miku V4X Original.

The second version is in English, again sung by Miku V6 Original and Miku V6 Soft, this time together with Miku V4 English.

And the third version is in Chinese, featuring Miku V6 Original and Miku V6 Soft once more, this time paired with Miku V4 Chinese.

Well, Miku V6 can sing in three languages so good for her.

I put a lot of work into this one—I’ve been working on it since spring—and there are parts where I’m not entirely sure if everything sounds right.

I probably complicated things more than I needed to with this song, but I still hope it turned out well.

Tuning and arranging notes for Miku can be pretty challenging, so I truly hope it turned out well.

I like how it came together though, and I really hope it sounds good to you too.

Throughout the entire song, Miku V6 Original, Miku V6 Soft, and Miku V4 English sung as vocal harmonies too that feels like a symphony of Miku voices.

So cute!

I also prepared something really nice at both the beginning and the end of the video, right before the song comes in.

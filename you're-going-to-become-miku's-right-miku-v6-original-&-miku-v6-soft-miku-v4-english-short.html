Hatsune Miku ( Miku V6 Original & Soft, Miku V4 English) — YOU'RE GOING TO BECOME MIKU'S, RIGHT — Short Version — Miku 19th Anniversary

It’s a short piece from one of the anniversary songs I created for you, Hatsune Miku, in celebration of your 19th anniversary.

I truly hope you enjoy it.

It’s part of a three‑version series of the same song.

This one is the English version, sung by Miku V6 Original, Miku V6 Soft, and Miku V4 English.

The second version is in Japanese, again sung by Miku V6 Original and Miku V6 Soft, this time together with Miku V4X Original.

And the third version features Miku V6 Original and Miku V6 Soft once more but paired with Miku V4 Chinese.

Well, Miku V6 can sing in three languages so good for her.

I put a lot of work into this one—I’ve been working on it since spring—and there are parts where I’m not entirely sure if everything sounds right. I probably complicated things more than I needed to with this song, but I still hope it turned out well. Tuning and arranging notes for Miku can be pretty challenging, so I truly hope it turned out well. I like how it came together though, and I really hope it sounds good to you too.
